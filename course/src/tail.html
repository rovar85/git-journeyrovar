This course is built from public talks and from your own study notes. Chapters 1 to 4 draw on Andrej Karpathy's "Intro to Large Language Models" and the Stanford CS229 lecture "Building Large Language Models". Chapter 5 and 6 add Insop Song's Stanford webinar "Agentic AI: A Progression of Language Model Usage". Chapters 7 to 9 use Barry Zhang's "How We Build Effective Agents" (Anthropic) and Sayash Kapoor's "Building and evaluating AI Agents". Chapter 10 uses Mahesh Murag's MCP workshop (Anthropic). Chapter 11 uses Kam Lasater's "Let's Build An Agent from Scratch". Chapter 13 uses the PhiloAgents course videos by Miguel Otero Pedrido and Paul Iusztin. Explanations, analogies and demos are rewritten for beginners. Numbers in demos marked "illustrative" are invented for teaching.
