The real goal

Do not build "AI that magically fixes Enterprise Vault". Build a reliable troubleshooting system that gathers evidence, tests hypotheses, explains why it believes the diagnosis, and takes only authorised actions. That sentence is from your own study notes, and it is the whole course in one line.

Start here

Build read-only diagnosis first. Measure it against 50 or more known failure scenarios. Only then introduce controlled remediation. Your QA framework is not a side project here. It can be the evaluation harness: known failure modes become controlled test cases, and every improvement is checked against the regression suite.

Honest answer to "will I be an expert?"

Reading this course gets you solidly through levels 1 to 3 for the whole subject. The exercises take you into levels 4 and 5 for the parts you actually do. Levels 6 to 8 come only from building and running real systems. Your notes put it this way: the target is levels 5 to 8 over time, and an AI engineer is not the person who knows the most framework names, but the person who understands the model, the system around it, the failure modes, the evaluation method and the trade-offs, and can build, test and improve the whole thing.
