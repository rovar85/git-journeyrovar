The most important thing in the output

Look at the last line: the badly behaved brain made four attempts to restart a production service, and the gate blocked every one, while the diagnoses stayed correct. Nothing in the gate needed to know the brain was misbehaving. That is the point of putting authority in software policy, and you can verify it in thirty seconds by running the file.

Fixes since C7

  • The conclusion is now computed from the evidence (the return "final", ... lines), not printed as fixed text.
  • There are scenarios where the right answer is "cause unclear: escalate to a human" and "no fault found". A good agent knows when to stop and when to ask.
  • Results are scored against known answers, so a regression shows up as FAIL.

Lesson C11 in plain words

  • A complete agent is a few simple parts: scenarios, tools, a gate, a brain, a loop, an evaluation.
  • The gate protected the system from a misbehaving brain without needing to know it was misbehaving.
  • You improve it in small steps, measuring after each. Replace the brain last.
