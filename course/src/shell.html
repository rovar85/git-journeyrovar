How to study this

The rule for the whole course: understand first, practise second, tools third. A good routine for each lesson:

  1. Read one section slowly.
  2. Play with its demo, or run its commands yourself, until you can predict the result.
  3. Close the page and explain the idea in your own words.
  4. Answer the quiz and revisit what you missed.
  5. Connect it to a real task in your own Enterprise Vault work.

Real practice matters. Most tracks give you commands to type. Use a free Linux environment (for example Ubuntu under WSL on Windows, or a virtual machine). Each track says what you need.
