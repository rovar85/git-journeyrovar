How to study this

The rule for the whole course: understand first, code second, framework third. Do not rush. A good daily ritual looks like this:

  1. Read one section slowly.
  2. Play with its demo until you can predict what it will do.
  3. Close the page and explain the idea in your own words, out loud or on paper.
  4. Answer the "Student asks" questions before reading the replies.
  5. Connect the idea to one real example from your own work.
  6. Only then move on.
